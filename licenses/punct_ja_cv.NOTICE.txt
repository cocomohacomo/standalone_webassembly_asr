configs/punct_ja_cv.tsv — punctuation n-gram count table (Phase 14)

Derived from the Japanese sentences of Mozilla Common Voice 8.0 (test split
as packaged by japanese-asr/ja_asr.common_voice_8_0), which are released
under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). The 200 rows used for evaluation
were excluded from training. The table itself is also dedicated CC0 1.0.
